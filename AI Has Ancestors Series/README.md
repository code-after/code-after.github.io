# AI Has Ancestors Series

*The public lead-in to **Code After Language**, the first paper in the Code After Series.*

> Artificial intelligence is not the first technology to think — it is the fifth member of a family that has been reshaping how humanity stores, transmits, and performs thought for five thousand years. This series tells that family's story, and asks what the newest member's arrival means for the languages, institutions, and societies now living in its shadow.

This folder collects the essays of the **AI Has Ancestors** series — the public introduction to the Code After research programme's first paper, **Code After Language** (Zenodo, September 2026). Written in British spelling throughout. The full framework, the primary manuscript, and the rest of the series live at [codeafter.ai](https://codeafter.ai).

## Series Vocabulary

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Pre-Code condition** | Every technology that worked on the record of thought and left the thinking to us (writing, alphabet, printing, digital wire). |
| **Post-Code condition** | The fifth member's state: a technology that performs thought rather than working on its record. |
| **Awareness Asymmetry** | The gap between societies where AI is a present-tense fact and those where it arrives as a future tense from elsewhere. |
| **Tail-chasing** | AI's uneven reshaping of work — deleting relevance at the low end while raising the stakes at the high end. |

## About Code After

**Code After** is a global, multilingual research programme examining how artificial intelligence reshapes the institutional structures of everyday life. The programme's first paper, **Code After Language** (due September 2026), examines what the arrival of a machine that performs thought asks of every language now living in its shadow. The AI Has Ancestors series is its public lead-in.
